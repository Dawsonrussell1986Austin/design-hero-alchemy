The Oak Companies / Roswell
A private evening reception / Invitation only

The lending platform institutions rely on. Now open to your questions.

The Oak Companies is not a lender in the traditional sense. It is a commercial real estate lending platform that has defragmented a market with more than $300 billion in annual demand — originating, underwriting, funding and servicing senior loans for insurance companies, foundations and endowments. LENS™, Oak's proprietary underwriting and monitoring technology, is in market today, working with those same institutions and their investment platforms in real time. Join Kevin Kennedy and Brook Scardina to see how the platform works, then bring your questions directly to them.
